About Climax Coaches

Safe, comfortable travel across Western Kenya.

Who we are

Climax Coaches is a Kenyan intercity bus company founded in 2008 that began with two buses and has grown into one of the recognisable long-distance operators serving Western Kenya, Nyanza and parts of the Rift Valley.

We run daily services from Nairobi to towns including Busia, Malaba, Kisii, Kisumu, Kitale, Kakamega and Bungoma. Book online, pay with M-Pesa and receive your instant ticket on WhatsApp.
