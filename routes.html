Frequently asked questions

How do I book a Climax bus online?

Choose your route, date and departure time, pick your seats, enter each passenger's details and confirm on WhatsApp. It takes about two minutes.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa payment instructions are sent to you on WhatsApp when you confirm your booking.

How do I get my ticket?

Your ticket is sent on WhatsApp after your booking is confirmed and paid.

Are fares fixed?

Fares shown are per passenger, one way. They may change with demand and season, so the fare confirmed on WhatsApp applies.
